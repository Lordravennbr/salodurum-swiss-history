# Source Policy

## Purpose

Every historical chapter, map, timeline, biography, archaeological entry, and reconstruction in Salodurum Swiss History must be traceable to evidence.

## Required source record

For each source used, record when available:

- author or responsible institution;
- title;
- publication or collection;
- publication date;
- edition or report number;
- page, section, catalogue number, excavation context, or passage when relevant;
- stable URL, DOI, ISBN, archival reference, or museum catalogue identifier;
- access date for online sources;
- language;
- rights or reuse status for visual assets.

## Historical claims

Citations should be placed as close as practical to the claim they support. A bibliography alone is not sufficient for controversial, precise, or uncertain claims.

## Conflicting sources

When credible sources disagree, the project must document the disagreement and identify the competing interpretations. It must not silently select one interpretation as fact.

## Unknowns

Absence of evidence must not be converted into evidence of absence unless the underlying scholarship supports that conclusion.

The approved wording for unresolved questions is explicit: **Insufficient evidence to establish this claim.**

## Visual sources

Maps, photographs, excavation drawings, museum objects, manuscripts, and archival images require a rights check before repository inclusion. A public web page does not imply that an image is free to redistribute.

## Source folders

Source documentation should be organized under:

- `sources/primary/`
- `sources/archaeological/`
- `sources/academic/`
- `sources/institutional/`
- `sources/bibliography/`

## Research status

Unresolved research belongs under `research/insufficient-evidence/` or `research/disputed/` rather than being inserted into the verified historical narrative.
