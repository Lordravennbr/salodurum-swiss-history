# Attribution — Sonic Vantage Bibliotec

## Project

**Salodurum Swiss History**  
A Sonic Vantage Bibliotec (SVB) research project.

## Preferred attribution

When reusing original material from this repository, please credit it as:

> Sonic Vantage Bibliotec (SVB), *Salodurum Swiss History*, CC BY 4.0.

When practical, attribution should also identify the specific chapter, map, illustration, dataset, or document used and link back to this repository.

## Adapted material

If you modify, translate, redraw, remix, or otherwise adapt original SVB material, clearly indicate that changes were made. Do not imply that Sonic Vantage Bibliotec endorses the adaptation.

## Third-party material

The CC BY 4.0 license for this repository does not automatically apply to third-party material.

Every third-party photograph, archaeological plan, historical map, archival document, quotation, dataset, illustration, or other asset must retain its original attribution and licensing information. If the reuse status of an asset cannot be verified, it must not be redistributed by this project.

## Historical-source attribution

Credit to Sonic Vantage Bibliotec never replaces citation of the historians, archaeologists, museums, archives, universities, government archaeological services, primary sources, or other institutions whose work supports a historical claim.

Historical claims in this library must remain traceable to their underlying sources.
